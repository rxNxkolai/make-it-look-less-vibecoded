Four bakes, then we stop.

We mill nothing and grow nothing. Flour comes from a mill two counties over, in paper sacks, on a Tuesday. The starter came from Ruth's mother and lives in a bucket by the door, which is the warmest corner of the room.

Dough is mixed at 21:00 and ferments cold overnight. Shaping starts at 04:00 and is done by hand, which is why no two loaves weigh the same and why we weigh yours at the till. When the last tray goes, the board reads zero and we close the door.
