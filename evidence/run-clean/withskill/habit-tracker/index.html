What you get for a mark.

01One sheet a year
Every habit you keep lives on the same page. You scroll a year sideways instead of tapping through twelve month screens looking for April.
02Marks, not scores
A day is marked or it is not. Notch holds no points, no levels, and no weekly grade. There is nothing in the app that can go up.
03Gaps stay visible
An empty square is data, so it stays on the sheet. You can see the fortnight you fell off and the Tuesday you came back, side by side, for as long as you keep the sheet.
04Offline first
Marks are written to the device before anything is sent anywhere. A morning underground still counts, and syncs when you surface.
05Made to print
Any sheet exports to a single page of A4 or Letter at 300 dpi, in black ink or in two colours. It is sized to pin above a desk.
06Export and leave
One button writes your whole history to CSV, one row per mark. You do not need an account, or us, to read it back.

On the day you miss

The counter does not reset, because there is no counter.

Most trackers treat an empty day as a failure to be cleared up. The number goes back to zero, the flame goes out, and a notification arrives asking where you have been. What that teaches is to stop opening the app after a bad week.

Notch leaves the square empty and carries on. A year with eleven gaps in it is still a year of work, and it should look like one when you read it back in December.

A gap is information. Erasing it will not make you more consistent. The one rule Notch is built on

Questions people ask.

Do I need an account?

Not to try it. Marks live on the device until you ask for sync, and you can export them without ever giving an email address.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your sheets stay readable and exportable. Sync stops, and habits above the free limit of three go read-only. Nothing is deleted.

Is there a phone app?

The web app installs to the home screen and works offline. Native builds for iOS and Android are being written and are not ready yet.

Can I bring marks from another tracker?

Yes, by CSV with one row per mark. The import screen has a template and will tell you which rows it could not read, with the line numbers.

Does it send reminders?

One a day, at a time you pick, or none at all. There is no second nudge and no notification about your streak.

What can you see?

Habit names and mark dates, only when sync is on, and only to move them between your devices. Nothing is sold and there are no third-party trackers in the app.
